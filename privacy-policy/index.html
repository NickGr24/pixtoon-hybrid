02 Processing principles

2.1

We process your data in compliance with the principles of lawfulness, fairness and transparency, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, as well as integrity and confidentiality, in accordance with Law no. 195/2024.

03 Categories of data we process

3.1

Data you provide to us directly:

  1. through the contact form: your name, e-mail address, phone number, the content of your message and the files you attach;
  2. through subscription to the newsletter: your e-mail address (and, where applicable, your name);
  3. through direct communication with us (e-mail, phone): identification and contact details and the information you share with us.
3.2

Data collected automatically when you visit the Site:

  1. IP address, device and browser type, operating system, online identifiers and cookies;
  2. data on browsing and use of the Site (pages visited, length of visit, referral source), collected in particular through web analytics tools such as Google Analytics / Google Tag Manager.
3.3

We do not seek to collect special categories of data (sensitive data) through the Site. Please do not send us such data through the contact form or as attachments.

04 Purposes and legal grounds of processing

4.1

We process your data for the following purposes and on the following legal grounds:

  1. to respond to requests sent through the contact form or by other means and to take steps at your request prior to entering into a possible contract – ground: pre-contractual measures and/or our legitimate interest in communicating with interested persons;
  2. to send commercial communications and newsletters – ground: your consent, which you may withdraw at any time;
  3. to ensure the operation, security and maintenance of the Site – ground: our legitimate interest;
  4. to analyse and improve the Site through analytics cookies – ground: your consent, given through the cookie banner;
  5. to comply with our legal obligations – ground: legal obligation;
  6. to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim – ground: our legitimate interest.

05 Cookies

5.1

The Site uses cookies and similar technologies. Cookies that are not strictly necessary (including analytics and marketing cookies) are used only on the basis of your consent, given through the cookie banner.

5.2

Details on the types of cookies, their purpose, duration and how to manage your preferences are set out in the Cookie Policy, available on the Site.

06 Recipients of the data

6.1

We may disclose your data to the following categories of recipients, only to the extent necessary:

  1. service providers acting as processors on behalf of the controller, under contracts that ensure data protection, such as: the web hosting provider, the e-mail/newsletter service provider and the providers of web analytics tools (for example, Google);
  2. public authorities, courts or other competent bodies, where we have a legal obligation to do so;
  3. professional advisers (legal, accounting), where necessary.
6.2

We do not sell your personal data and do not disclose it to third parties for purposes other than those set out in this Policy.

07 Transfers outside the Republic of Moldova

7.1

Some of our service providers (for example, web analytics or e-mail providers) may process data on servers located outside the Republic of Moldova, including in third countries such as the United States of America.

7.2

In such cases, we ensure that the transfer is carried out in compliance with Law no. 195/2024, on the basis of appropriate safeguards (for example, standard contractual clauses or other mechanisms provided by law) or in one of the situations permitted by law.

08 Data retention period

8.1

We keep your data only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, as follows:

  1. data from contact requests: for the duration of handling the request and, thereafter, for a period of 3 years (for example, to comply with limitation periods), after which it is deleted or anonymised;
  2. newsletter subscription data: until consent is withdrawn (unsubscription);
  3. data collected through cookies: for the validity period of each cookie, as indicated in the Cookie Policy;
  4. data required to comply with legal obligations: for the period provided by the applicable legislation.

09 Your rights

9.1

In accordance with Law no. 195/2024, you have the following rights:

  1. the right of access to your data;
  2. the right to rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  3. the right to erasure of data (“the right to be forgotten”), under the conditions provided by law;
  4. the right to restriction of processing;
  5. the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest and to processing for direct marketing purposes;
  6. the right to data portability;
  7. the right to withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal;
  8. the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing which produces legal effects or similarly significantly affects you.
9.2

You can exercise these rights by sending a request to hello@pixtoon.com. We will respond to your request within the time limits provided by law.

9.3

If you consider that the processing of your data breaches the legislation, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority: the National Centre for Personal Data Protection (CNPDCP), Chișinău, str. Serghei Lazo 48, MD-2004, Republic of Moldova; e-mail: centru@datepersonale.md; web: www.datepersonale.md.

10 Data security

10.1

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, destruction, alteration or unauthorised disclosure. However, the transmission of information over the internet cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11 Processing of minors’ data

11.1

The Site is addressed to persons with full legal capacity and is not intended for minors. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe that a minor has provided us with data without the necessary consent, please contact us so that we can remove it.

12 Automated decisions and profiling

12.1

We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing of data that produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you. Web analytics tools may process data in aggregated form, for statistical purposes.

13 Changes to this Policy

13.1

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version is published on the Site, indicating the date of the last update, and takes effect from the date of publication. We recommend that you review it periodically.
