This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which types of cookies we use on the website www.pixtoon.com (hereinafter “the Site”), for what purposes, on what legal basis, and how you can manage your preferences. The Policy supplements the Privacy Policy of PIXTOON ANIMATION SRL and applies in accordance with Law no. 195/2024 on the protection of personal data and related legislation.

01 What cookies are

1.1

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, phone, tablet) when you visit a website. They allow the device to be recognised and retain certain information about your preferences or actions.

1.2

We also use similar technologies (for example pixels, tags and scripts such as Google Tag Manager). In this Policy, all of these are referred to generically as “cookies”.

02 Legal basis and consent

2.1

Cookies strictly necessary for the operation of the Site are used on the basis of our legitimate interest in ensuring the provision and security of the Site and do not require consent.

2.2

The other categories of cookies (analytics, marketing and functional) are used only on the basis of your consent, given through the cookie banner displayed on your first visit to the Site. You may withdraw your consent at any time through the preferences panel or through your browser settings.

03 Categories of cookies we use

3.1

Depending on their purpose, we use the following categories of cookies:

  1. Strictly necessary cookies – ensure the basic operation of the Site and remember your cookie preferences; they cannot be switched off in our systems.
  2. Analytics / performance cookies – help us understand how the Site is used (pages visited, length of visit) in order to improve it; they are used on the basis of consent.
  3. Marketing / advertising cookies – used to measure the effectiveness of campaigns and, where applicable, to display relevant content; they are used on the basis of consent.
  4. Functional cookies – remember your preferences (for example, language) for a personalised experience; they are used on the basis of consent.

04 Third-party cookies and services

4.1

The Site uses third-party services that may place cookies, in particular Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, provided by Google. These services may process data, including your IP address, for analytics purposes.

4.2

The use of Google services may involve the transfer of data outside the Republic of Moldova, including to the United States of America, subject to the safeguards provided by law, as described in the Privacy Policy.

4.3

You can learn more about how Google processes data and use the opt-out module for Google Analytics by visiting Google’s dedicated pages.

05 Duration of cookies

5.1

Depending on their lifetime, cookies can be: (a) session cookies – deleted automatically when the browser is closed; and (b) persistent cookies – remain on the device for the period indicated in the tables above or until you delete them.

06 How to manage your preferences

6.1

On your first visit to the Site, you can accept or refuse the non-essential categories of cookies through the cookie banner. You can change your choices at any time through the preferences panel available on the Site.

6.2

You can also manage or delete cookies from your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the operation of the Site.

07 Changes to this Policy

7.1

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The updated version is published on the Site, indicating the date of the last update.
