01 — AI hybrid / 3D character / Product / Environment

Drive Gas

A branded 3D world and character-driven commercial created through a hybrid AI + animation pipeline.

Challenge

What we had to solve.

Give a fuel retailer a loyalty campaign that reads as a story rather than a discount banner — and put a recognisable face on the brand.

Solution

How we combined filming, AI and post-production.

We built a branded 3D world and a host character to carry it. AI handled environment and product visuals; the character performance and brand geometry stayed hand-directed.

Production

How the project was made.

Concept and script in-house, character design and 3D development, AI-assisted environments and product shots, animation, compositing, sound design and final master.

Result

The finished film.

A campaign world that can be re-entered for the next promo instead of being rebuilt from zero.
