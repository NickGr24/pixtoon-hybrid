How hybrid production works

AI is part of the pipeline. Not the director.

We combine traditional creative production with the latest AI tools to produce high-quality commercial content faster and more efficiently.

  1. 01

    Brief

    We learn about the brand, product, objective and audience.

  2. 02

    Concept

    Pixtoon develops the creative idea, script and visual direction.

  3. 03

    AI production

    AI is used where it makes production faster or opens creative possibilities: characters, environments, product visuals, shots and visual development.

  4. 04

    Animation & post

    Our team handles animation, compositing, editing, voice, music, sound design and final polish.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Final commercial video delivered for social media, digital ads, YouTube, TV and DOOH.
