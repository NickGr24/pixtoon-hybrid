Continuity is the advantage

Build the world once. Keep telling new stories.

The advantage of monthly production is not simply “more videos for less money”. It is continuity.

Once Pixtoon has developed the brand’s visual world — the characters or mascot, the visual style, the tone and the recurring formats — we can keep producing new content far faster and hold a consistent brand universe, month after month.

Case study — Microinvest

The Microinvest family.
Built by hand. Animated with AI.

The Microinvest family characters are built by hand — modelled in every pose, expression and detail a brand mascot needs. The AI is then trained on those characters, and the content is created with it — far faster, far more of it, with the identity kept intact. AI animation needs no rig.

Character built by hand

The Microinvest family — modelled by hand in every pose, expression and detail, as a complete brand mascot.

AI trained on the character

Once the character is ready, the AI is trained on it. Then it generates new content — no rig, straight from what it learned.

Content volume

Why it works

Hand-built character + trained AI = real scalability

A character built by hand — in every pose, expression and detail — has an identity and a consistency that AI alone cannot reproduce.

Once the AI is trained on that character, we produce new scenes, reactions, movements and stories at a pace nothing else could reach — no rig, straight from what it learned.

How hybrid production works

AI is part of the pipeline.
Not the director.

We combine traditional creative production with the newest AI tools to produce high-quality commercial content faster and more efficiently.

  1. 01

    Brief

    We learn the brand, the product, the objective and the audience.

  2. 02

    Concept

    Pixtoon develops the creative idea, the script and the visual direction.

  3. 03

    AI production

    AI accelerates characters, environments, product visuals and frames — wherever it opens up creative possibilities.

  4. 04

    Animation & post

    Our team handles animation, compositing, editing, voice, music, sound design and final finishing.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Final video delivered for social, digital ads, YouTube, TV and DOOH.
