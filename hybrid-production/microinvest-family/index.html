04 — Rigged 3D characters / AI animation

Familia Microinvest

A family of characters built the classic way — fully modelled and rigged — and now animated with AI tools.

Challenge

What we had to solve.

Give a financial brand a cast of characters that stays recognisable across years of campaigns, not a one-off commercial.

Solution

How we combined filming, AI and post-production.

The Microinvest family was modelled and rigged in classic 3D, with full animation controllers. That base is now driven by AI animation, so new scenes cost a fraction of a traditional shoot.

Production

How the project was made.

Classic 3D modelling and rigging in-house, AI-assisted animation, compositing, sound and final master.

Result

The finished film.

A brand universe that can be extended continuously — reels, social spots and brand scenes — without rebuilding the characters each time.
