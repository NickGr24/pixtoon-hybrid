Familia Microinvest

A family that explains finance simply.

We built a close, believable and expressive family that lives through real financial situations from everyday life.

The characters speak directly, clearly and in terms everyone understands.

Where we work classically.
Where AI speeds us up.

  1. 01

    Concept & design

    We define the personalities, the look and the world the family lives in.

  2. 02

    Classic 3D build

    We model and rig the characters with full control over their identity.

  3. 03

    Sets & props

    We build the environments and key objects for coherence and storytelling.

  4. 04

    AI-assisted animation

    We use AI to generate motion, variations and iterations at scale.

  5. 05

    Compositing & colour

    We finish the image, the light and the rhythm for publication-ready deliverables.

Human creative control

  • Design, proportions and expressions 100% controlled.
  • Brand coherence in every frame.
  • Creative decisions signed off by people.

AI acceleration

  • More iterations in less time.
  • A range of motion and situations generated fast.
  • Optimised cost, with no compromise on identity.
