Case study — Microinvest

The Microinvest Family

From three characters to a world that can grow every month.

Long before today's generative tools existed, Pixtoon built Microinvest a whole family: a mother, a father and a daughter.

a frame from one of the family's episodes ♡

Client
Microinvest
Objective
Monthly content
Production
Classic 3D + hybrid
Format
Up to 2 spots a month

What makes hybrid production possible.

  • Characters we reuse

    We don't start from zero for every episode.

  • A consistent identity

    The same characters, the same visual world.

  • A monthly flow

    Up to two spots a month, on new subjects.

  • A world that grows

    Sets, situations and characters get added over time.

The family was built by hand, character by character.

We developed the characters and used them across a run of campaigns — some comic, some educational — where financial products could be explained in terms close to people's own lives.

In time the characters became more than the heroes of a single spot. They became a recognisable identity for how Microinvest speaks.

A new stage for the same family

As the Pixtoon hybrid pipeline developed, we proposed turning the family into a recurring format: up to two spots a month, with new episodes, new situations and new campaigns.

  • We are no longer limited to the same house.
  • We brought in a cat.
  • We created a second family.
  • We can widen the world around them step by step.

We choose the method
shot by shot.

  1. 01

    Concept & characters

    We define the story, the personalities and the world it happens in.

  2. 02

    Classic 3D build

    We model and texture whatever has to stay identical in every shot.

  3. 03

    Sets & props

    We build the environments and objects that carry the story.

  4. 04

    Hybrid production

    The rest of the shots use faster methods, chosen scene by scene.

  5. 05

    Compositing & finish

    Everything arrives in one language: light, colour, rhythm, sound.

From a campaign to a brand series

Instead of starting from zero every time, Microinvest can build a world that develops along with the brand.

The characters can teach, entertain, explain products and come back in short formats, themed episodes or seasonal campaigns. Every Microinvest product can become the subject of an episode, without audiences feeling they are watching one more unconnected ad.

Where we work in classic 3D

  • The product and the character stay exactly as approved.
  • Full control over movement, proportions and expressions.
  • The shots where precision matters most.

Where we work hybrid

  • New scenes, situations and variants in far less time.
  • The budget concentrates on the shots that carry the film.
  • Content that can keep going month after month.

Characters built once. Content every month. The same world, steadily bigger.

  • Characters that have already been developed get reused.
  • The visual identity stays consistent.
  • New sets, situations and characters come together quickly.
  • A monthly flow of content becomes possible.
  • Episodes adapt to different products and campaigns.
  • A long-term relationship builds between audiences and characters.

This model can work for your brand if:

  • You announce new products or offers every month.
  • You want characters audiences will recognise.
  • You need both commercial and educational content.
  • You want to build a series, not a collection of unrelated spots.
