02 — AI character / Product commercial

Coccolino

AI-assisted character and product advertising built around the existing Coccolino brand universe.

Challenge

What we had to solve.

Work inside a brand universe that already exists, with a mascot audiences know — nothing could look reinterpreted or off-model.

Solution

How we combined filming, AI and post-production.

AI generation was constrained to the established look: the bear, the palette and the product stayed on-model, while AI opened up settings that would have needed a full shoot.

Production

How the project was made.

Creative concept, visual development against brand guidelines, AI image and video generation, animation and compositing, editing, music and sound design.

Result

The finished film.

Product advertising that sits inside the existing brand world rather than beside it.
