03 — AI animated series / Brand characters

Medpark — Doctor Buba

A branded animated content universe created around Medpark's characters.

Challenge

What we had to solve.

Make a private clinic approachable for children and parents — a subject where tone matters more than production value.

Solution

How we combined filming, AI and post-production.

A cast of brand characters and a repeatable series format. Once the world was designed, each new episode starts from an existing set, not from a blank page.

Production

How the project was made.

Character and world design, series format, script per episode, AI-assisted production, 2D and 3D animation, voice, music and sound design.

Result

The finished film.

A content universe that keeps producing episodes instead of a single finished spot.
